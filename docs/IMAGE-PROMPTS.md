# Velvet — Photography Prompts

The prototype uses original SVG cake illustrations as stand-ins. For the marketplace version, replace them with
photography that you own or license. Use AI generation with commercial rights, a shoot, or licensed stock.

Keep everything in one consistent style:
- warm side-light, soft shadows, cream/oat backdrops
- the pistachio + strawberry + cocoa palette
- shallow depth of field

**Cut-out product shots** need a transparent background (PNG/WebP), square 1600×1600, with the product filling
about 80% of the frame and centred on a white cake stand. Remove the background after generating.
