Home / Our story

Started with one oven.

Velvet Crumb began in 2019 as a Saturday market stall with a borrowed oven and one pistachio cake recipe. It still starts at 5am, every day.

We believe a cake should taste as good as it looks — so we bake with French butter, free-range eggs, fruit from farms we can drive to, and the patience to let things rest.

Timeline

How we rose.

2019

The market stall

Twelve cakes a week at Greenpoint Farmers Market. The Pistachio Rose sells out by 10am.

2020

Doorstep deliveries

Lockdown birthdays become our thing. We deliver 3,000 cakes by bike.

2021

Mercer Lane

Our first studio opens, with a window onto the bakery so you can watch the piping.

2023

First wedding season

Forty-two weddings, one five-tier showstopper, and a lot of sugar roses.

2025

West Village & shipping

A second studio, plus frozen nationwide shipping to all 48 states.

Now

Still one recipe book

Every cake still comes from Mara's handwritten notebook.

Our values

What goes in.

real butter

No shortcuts

No premixes, no shortening, no artificial colours. Our pink comes from raspberries.

close to home

Local first

Dairy from the Hudson Valley, fruit from farms within 150 miles, eggs from a farm we've visited.

for everyone

Clear allergens

Every bake is labelled, and we keep gluten-free and vegan options on the menu every day.
