FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Message us — we reply within one working day.

How far ahead should I order?
Most cakes need 48 hours. Cakes labelled "Same day" can be ordered until 12pm for delivery that afternoon. Custom and tiered cakes need at least 3 weeks.
Do you have gluten-free or vegan cakes?
Yes. Filter by dietary needs in the shop. Our kitchen handles gluten, nuts, eggs and dairy, so we can't guarantee zero cross-contact.
Can you write a message on the cake?
Yes, up to 30 characters, free of charge, for pickup and local delivery. Nationwide shipped cakes come with a gift card instead.
How does nationwide shipping work?
Cakes ship frozen Monday to Wednesday and arrive in 1–2 days. Thaw in the fridge for 5–6 hours before serving. Choose a delivery date 1–3 days before your event.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Up to 48 hours before pickup or delivery (72 hours for 8"+ and tiered cakes). Wedding deposits are non-refundable but can move to a new date once.
Do you deliver to venues and set up?
Yes, for tiered cakes within 30 miles. Setup starts at $85 and includes a cake stand loan.
