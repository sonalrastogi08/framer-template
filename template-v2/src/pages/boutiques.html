Questions

Asked at midnight.

How far ahead should I order?
Entremets need 48 hours. Pieces marked "Same day" can be ordered until 12:00 for delivery that evening. Wedding and tiered commissions need four weeks.
Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?
Yes. Use the dietary filters in the collection. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy and eggs, so we can't promise zero cross-contact.
How do shipped entremets arrive?
Frozen, in recyclable insulation, within two days. Thaw in the fridge for six hours. Mirror glazes stay glossy through freezing.
Can I add a message?
Yes, up to 24 characters on a chocolate plaque, free of charge. Shipped coffrets come with a handwritten card instead.
Can I book the dessert bar for a private evening?
Yes, Mondays and Tuesdays, for up to 20 guests. Use the commission form and choose "Something else".
What if my order is late or damaged?
Write to us within 24 hours with a photo and we'll remake or refund it.
