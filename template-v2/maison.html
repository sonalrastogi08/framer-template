Cerise/Maison

Born after hours.

We believe dessert deserves its own evening. So we bake in the afternoon, open when dinner ends, and never sell anything made the day before.

2019
Chapter one

A supper club in a walk-up

Inès Moreau, a restaurant pastry chef, starts serving one dessert at midnight to twelve friends a week. It's always the cherry dome.

Chapter two

Orchard Street opens

A 14-seat dessert bar with a counter facing the kitchen. Open 14:00 to midnight, closed Mondays and Tuesdays.

Chapter three

Brooklyn & beyond

House rules

Three things we never do.

i.Sell anything baked the day before. Leftovers go to the night staff and a shelter on Delancey.
ii.Use artificial colour. Our red comes from cherries and beetroot, our gold is real leaf.
iii.Hide an allergen. Every piece lists all 14, in the boutique and online.
iv.Rush a glaze. A mirror glaze sets for four hours, so we plan around it.
